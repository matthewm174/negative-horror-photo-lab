Purpose of the repo:

To make a completely realistic photograpy process that specializes in lofi and antiquated photo development techniques and allows users to upload a simple negative of photography and do things like short or long exposure, as well as mis-lightings that blur things out. every layer has to be placed in front of an aperture to be printed to the piece of paper. there are also different emulsifying techniques and mediums that make this have different results and hues. 

types would be like:

camera obscura

heliography

daguerrotype
calotype

silver gelatine dry plates 


things like chemical noise and uneven distribution would be used to help create eerie effects.
